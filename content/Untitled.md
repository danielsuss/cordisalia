### Chapter 4 - Rorkard at Last

---

![[Pasted image 20240423003101.png]] After helping [[Elinor]] with the [[Boar|Boars]] and getting a good night's rest, the gang continues their journey through the [[Verdant Forest]]. The group discovers that the small sprite [[Liri]] has been following them since they freed her from the [[Kobold|Kobolds]]. She explains that she is curious about their quest and offers to accompany them, agreeing to help in any way she can with her ability to transform into small animals. [[Brogan Stone-Flask|Brogan]] is skeptical, but [[Ylvëstrïa Wuvarion|Woo]] and [[Ysgarlad]] welcome the little sprite as she perches on [[Ysgarlad]]'s shoulder.

The next day at noon, [[Liri]] starts frantically pointing ahead and the gang finally sees it - the town of [[Rorkard]] in the distance. ![[rorkard.webp]] As they approach the entrance to the settlement, they notice a fluffy grey cat with striking green eyes sitting in a tree, observing them with unusual attentiveness. Looking down to their right, they see children playing by the [[River Midwood]], skimming stones across the water's surface. Upon noticing the newcomers, the children look up, smile, and perform what appears to be a traditional [[Rorkard]] greeting - a wave with both hands followed by a small bow. Further along the riverbank sits a bald man with a fishing rod, calmly waiting for a bite.

```
Ysgarlad:
	Excuse me, my good man. We're looking for the Cruster Guild. Do you know where we might find it?
```

The bald man looks up, studies the group for a moment, then smiles.

```
???:
	Ah, newcomers. The Cruster Guild is in the northern district. Head through the market and follow the signs. Can't miss it. Be kind to our town, and our town will be kind to you.
```

The man returns to his fishing, and the gang thanks him before continuing into [[Rorkard]]. As they enter the town proper, they notice it's busier than expected, with various districts dedicated to different trades. They decide to split up temporarily - [[Ysgarlad]] and [[Liri]] will deliver [[Elinor]]'s cake to [[Sarmon]] at the [[Rocky Refuge]], while [[Brogan Stone-Flask|Brogan]] and [[Ylvëstrïa Wuvarion|Woo]] explore the market district.

### Plot 1 - Rocky Refuge Inn and the Cake Delivery

![[rorkard inn.webp]] [[Ysgarlad]] and [[Liri]] make their way to the [[Rocky Refuge]], following directions from various townsfolk. The inn is a large, two-story stone building with a thatched roof and several chimneys billowing smoke. As they push open the door, they're greeted by the warm ambiance of a bustling tavern - the scent of ale and stew, the sound of laughter and conversation, and the crackling of a large hearth fire.

At the bar stands a middle-aged man with a thick mustache and a friendly demeanor, serving drinks to patrons. [[Ysgarlad]] approaches him with the cake from [[Elinor]].

```
Ysgarlad:
	Are you Sarmon? I have a delivery for you from Elinor.
```

The man's eyes light up as he sees the cake.

```
Sarmon:
	Ah, Elinor's fruit cake! It's been too long since I've had some. She's well, I hope?
```

```
Ysgarlad:
	She's doing fine. We helped her with a boar problem at her cabin.
```

[[Sarmon]] laughs heartily.

```
Sarmon:
	Those boars are a persistent lot! Thank you for delivering this. Please, have a drink on the house.
```

As [[Ysgarlad]] and [[Liri]] enjoy their drinks, [[Sarmon]] introduces them to [[Gaffot]], a regular patron who claims to have "27 wives" and offers increasingly absurd stories about his adventures. During their conversation, [[Ysgarlad]] notices a woman with dark hair watching him from across the room. After a while, she approaches their table.

```
???:
	I haven't seen you around before. My name is Kiara. Are you an adventurer?
```

```
Ysgarlad:
	Just arrived today. I'm Ysgarlad, and my companions and I are on a quest.
```

[[Kiara]] smiles and sits down at their table uninvited.

```
Kiara:
	How fortunate. I've been looking for capable hands. In two days' time, I need help carrying crystals out of Gloomstone Cavern. The pay is good, and the work isn't dangerous - just tedious. Would you be interested?
```

[[Ysgarlad]] considers the offer, thinking it might provide some extra coin while they search for information about the [[Fragment of the Eternal Shadow]].

```
Ysgarlad:
	I'll discuss it with my companions, but it sounds reasonable. We'll let you know.
```

[[Kiara]] nods, thanks him, and returns to her table. [[Liri]] whispers to [[Ysgarlad]] that she doesn't trust the woman, but can't explain why.

### Plot 2 - The Market District

Meanwhile, [[Brogan Stone-Flask|Brogan]] and [[Ylvëstrïa Wuvarion|Woo]] are exploring the market district of [[Rorkard]]. The marketplace is vibrant, with vendors selling everything from fresh produce to crafted goods. As they wander through, they notice a small crowd gathered around a gambling stall.

```
???:
	Step right up! Test your luck and skill! Double your money or lose it all!
```

Curious, [[Brogan Stone-Flask|Brogan]] pushes his way to the front to see a man named [[Merek]] running a shell game. [[Merek]] is clearly making a good profit, and [[Brogan Stone-Flask|Brogan]] notices that he's using sleight of hand to cheat his customers. With a mischievous grin, [[Brogan Stone-Flask|Brogan]] nudges [[Ylvëstrïa Wuvarion|Woo]] and whispers a plan.

[[Brogan Stone-Flask|Brogan]] steps forward to play, placing a modest bet. As [[Merek]] begins moving the shells, [[Ylvëstrïa Wuvarion|Woo]] subtly uses her magic to mark the correct shell with a faint glow only [[Brogan Stone-Flask|Brogan]] can see. [[Brogan Stone-Flask|Brogan]] wins easily, much to [[Merek]]'s surprise.

```
Merek:
	Beginner's luck! Care to try again with a higher stake?
```

[[Brogan Stone-Flask|Brogan]] agrees, and they repeat the process several more times, gradually increasing the bet. By the time they're finished, they've won a substantial sum of gold, and [[Merek]] is looking increasingly agitated. As they walk away with their winnings, [[Ylvëstrïa Wuvarion|Woo]] notices the same grey cat from earlier watching them from atop a nearby roof.

The cat suddenly leaps down, catches a bird in mid-flight, and drops it at [[Ylvëstrïa Wuvarion|Woo]]'s feet. Looking closer, [[Ylvëstrïa Wuvarion|Woo]] sees that the bird has a small scroll tied to its leg. She takes the scroll and reads the message:

_"The one you seek rests within the inn."_

Below the text is a symbol [[Ylvëstrïa Wuvarion|Woo]] recognizes as the Eye of Mortem, confirming the message is from [[Post Mortem]]. She realizes they must be referring to the [[Hooded Man]].

### Plot 3 - Reuniting at the Cruster Guild

As evening approaches, the gang reunites outside the [[Cruster Guild]] as planned. The guild is a large, stone building with crystal-embedded columns at its entrance. Inside, they're surprised to find the bald fisherman from earlier, now dressed in formal attire and sitting behind a desk.

```
???:
	Ah, the newcomers! I'm Crystus, head of the Cruster Guild. I had a feeling you'd find your way here eventually.
```

[[Ysgarlad]] is momentarily embarrassed for not recognizing [[Crystus]] earlier, but the guild leader waves off any apology with good humor.

```
Crystus:
	So, what brings you to our humble town? We don't get many travelers this time of year.
```

[[Ysgarlad]] explains their mission to learn about the [[Fragment of the Eternal Shadow]], and [[Crystus]]'s expression turns serious.

```
Crystus:
	Are you familiar with the story of the Coalescence?
```

[[Crystus]] proceeds to tell them about [[The Coalescence]], the origin story of their world. He explains how the six brothers of the spiritual plane fused together to create Cordisalia, and in the process, fragments of the [[Eternal Shadow]] were scattered throughout the world.

```
Crystus:
	These fragments are rare, but they do exist. Some believe they hold immense power, waiting to be unlocked under the right conditions. Others think they're just remnants of a bygone era with no special properties. I've examined a few in my time, and they appear to be nothing more than purple crystals.
```

[[Ylvëstrïa Wuvarion|Woo]] asks [[Crystus]] about the [[Odd Stone]] she acquired from [[Hoagle]]. [[Crystus]] examines it and explains it's a [[Lodestone]], a type of ancient runic stone that can be used by an [[Arcane Smith]] to create specialized equipment.

While this conversation is happening, [[Brogan Stone-Flask|Brogan]] notices a small blue gem on display in a case nearby. When no one is looking, he pickpockets it, thinking it might make a nice gift for [[Liri]].

After their discussion with [[Crystus]], [[Ylvëstrïa Wuvarion|Woo]] shares the message from [[Post Mortem]] about the [[Hooded Man]] being at an inn. The gang decides to return to the [[Rocky Refuge]] to search for him.

### Plot 4 - Spirit Reader Shanya's

Before heading back to the inn, the gang notices a small, mystical-looking shop with a sign reading "Spirit Reader Shanya's." Curious, they decide to take a quick look. ![[Pasted image 20240319204045.png]] Upon entering, they find the shop empty, with a note on the counter stating that [[Shanya]] is out of town but will return soon. The interior is filled with magical artifacts, crystals, and potions. Despite the proprietor's absence, the gang makes a mental note to return later, as someone who reads spirits might be useful in their quest.

As they exit the shop and make their way back to the [[Rocky Refuge]], [[Ysgarlad]] fills the others in on [[Kiara]]'s offer. They agree it might be worth pursuing while they search for the [[Hooded Man]] and learn more about the [[Fragment of the Eternal Shadow]].

Night has fallen by the time they reach the inn, and the building is illuminated by warm light spilling from its windows. They enter, ready to find the man who set them on this journey and finally get some answers.
