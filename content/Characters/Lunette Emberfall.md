###### Overview
- [[Lunette Emberfall]] is a girl from [[Rorkard]] and the niece of [[Orson Emberfall]]
###### Appearances
###### [[Chapter 5 - Kiara's Deception]]
- Along with the gang and [[Kiara]] she went to [[Gloomstone Cavern]] to search for [[Orson Emberfall|Orson]]
- Ran away with [[Kiara]] when they trapped the gang with the [[Cannibals of Gloomstone Cavern]], however looking sorrowful
###### [[Chapter 7 - Morgrith's Final Banquet]]
- Was interrogated by [[Ysgarlad]] and [[Brogan Stone-Flask|Brogan]] in the [[Emberfall Household]] after being dusted with [[Aetherquartz Powder]]
- Revealed that she did in fact think that they were going to rescue [[Orson Emberfall|Orson]], but [[Kiara]] decided to spare her from the cannibals in the last moment
- Informs the gang of her once sister-like relationship with [[Kiara]], and bout her father's [[Abandoned Thieves Guild|hideout]]
