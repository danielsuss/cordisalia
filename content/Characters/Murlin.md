###### Overview
- [[Murlin]] is a "wizard" from the Stone-Flask family in the [[Burning Expanse]]
- His "magic" manifests as contraptions, gadgets, and tricks rather than traditional spellcasting
- Renamed himself after [[Merlin the Mystic]] from "The Chronicles of Merlin the Mystic," his favourite book
- Obsessed with finding resurrection magic to bring back his childhood friend [[Morgan A]], whom only he could see
