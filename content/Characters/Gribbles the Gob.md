###### Overview
- Gribbles was a man who had been trapped in [[The Shacklehold]] for over 5 years
- Gained the title "the Gob" due to his notoriety for talking on and on and on to great lengths about any subject - he was never sold due to this fact
###### [[Chapter 9.2 - Gribbles' Gambit]]
- Healed [[Wyatt Holloway|Wyatt]] after he was lashed by [[The Willbreaker]] upon his capture
- Decided that [[Mugwort Snaggletooth|Mugwort]] and [[Wyatt Holloway|Wyatt]] were worthy, and so decided to help them escape [[The Shacklehold]]
- Died to an arrow through the chest just as they were escaping over the bridge at [[Highvale Manor]]
- Trusted [[Wyatt Holloway|Wyatt]] and [[Mugwort Snaggletooth|Mugwort]] to come back and free the slaves with his dying words
