###### Overview
- [[Gronk Growheart]] is the overtly charismatic son of [[Grink Growheart]] and a man from [[Gilbreives]]
- He holds the record for most wins in the [[Lumber Festival]] wood chopping competition
- Popular with ladies, he has trouble keeping his manly urges under control 
- His wife is [[Rowena Growheart]], who is often annoyed at him for his repeated narcissism and misogyny
