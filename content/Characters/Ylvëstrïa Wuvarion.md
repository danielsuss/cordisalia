*High Elf Female 20 [[City of Virridius]]*
![[Woo.png]]
> *Woo baby, woo.*
###### Overview
- Her true origins unknown, [[Ylvëstrïa Wuvarion|Ylvëstrïa Wuvarion's]] first memories are from being raised in the slums of [[City of Virridius|Virridius]] around the age of five
- She discovered her natural acuity for magic when breaking into a mage's shop at age twelve
- Instead of reprimanding her, the mage, [[Magistrix Orinthe]] taught her how to harness the latent magical energy surrounding her
- After [[Magistrix Orinthe]] died of natural causes a few years later, [[Ylvëstrïa Wuvarion|Woo]] found herself homeless again
 - At this point, age fourteen, she caught the attention of the organisation [[Post Mortem]], who recognized something unusual in her magical signature and recruited her for her talents in infiltration
 - [[Post Mortem]] utilized her skills in breaking into secured locations to plant evidence or retrieve documents, while providing her with arcane texts in return. This relationship continued up until her recent departure from the [[City of Virridius]]
- Since leaving the [[City of Virridius|Virridius]], she has been adventuring independently, still relying on [[Post Mortem]] outposts as a reliable information source
- She maintains a personal code of justice despite her criminal activities, often targeting bullies and those who abuse power
