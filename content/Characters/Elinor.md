###### Overview
- [[Elinor]] is a woman who lives in a [[Elinor's Cabin|Cabin]] in the [[Verdant Forest]]
- An ex-adventurer, she has become highly adept at gardening in her later years, cultivating many rare and sought after plants
- She is friends with [[Hoagle]]
- She is famous among few for her delicious fruit cake
