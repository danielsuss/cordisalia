###### Overview
- [[Liri]] is a young sprite that has been travelling with the gang since they found her in the [[Verdant Forest]]
- Has the ability to turn into various creatures and critters
- Originally very nervous, she now has a very excited demeanour, and enjoys adventuring
###### Appearances
###### [[Chapter 3 - The Dangling Tradesman]]
- The gang found her trapped in a cage after killing the [[Kobold|Kobolds]] who stole [[Hoagle|Hoagle's]] bag
- [[Ysgarlad]] set her free however she came back to the gang after a day or so during their journey through the [[Verdant Forest]] towards [[Rorkard]] in search of food
- [[Liri]] decided to join the gang on their adventure after they showed her kindness
###### [[Chapter 4 - Rorkard at Last]]
- Turned into a boar to help the gang during their fight in [[Elinor|Elinor's]] Cabin
- She helped [[Ysgarlad]] deliver a cake to [[Sarmon]] as requested by [[Elinor]]
- She was given a blue gem by [[Brogan Stone-Flask|Brogan]] which he stole from the [[Cruster Guild]]
###### [[Chapter 5 - Kiara's Deception]]
- Got some much needed new clothes at the [[Ironhand Forge]]
- Had her spirit read by [[Shanya]], resulting in [[Shanya]] freaking out
###### [[Chapter 6 - Meat for Mother]]
- Transformed into a mouse to try and help the gang escape [[Gloomstone Cavern]] after their fight with the [[Cannibals of Gloomstone Cavern]]
- Was thrown by [[Ysgarlad]] to distract [[Morgrith's Servant|Mother's Servant]] as the gang were rescuing [[Amara]]
- Transformed into a bird to help the gang get back to [[Rorkard]] after escaping [[Gloomstone Cavern]]
- Fell unconscious after unleashing a mysterious purple blast in an effort to protect the gang from a group of [[Bugbear|Bugbears]]
###### [[Chapter 7 - Morgrith's Final Banquet]]
- Doesn't remember what happened with the [[Bugbear|Bugbears]] after asked by [[Ysgarlad]]
- Acts as a surveillance mouse when [[Brogan Stone-Flask|Brogan]] and [[Ysgarlad]] went to the [[Emberfall Household]]
- Does not remember [[Amara]] similar to the [[Bugbear|Bugbears]]
###### [[Chapter 8 - Surprise Attack!]]
- Touched the fragment of the eternal shadow, causing it to crack, releasing a powerful blast of purple energy, wrecking the interior of the [[Skogbrew Tavern]] and sending everyone bar herself flying
- The energy seemed to cling to her for a moment, her wings and eyes purple, with purple sparks around her body, before she fainted
###### [[Chapter 9 - Florion's Tears]]
- Continued to travel with the gang through [[Florion's Ridge]]
###### [[Chapter 10.2 - Enter! Wyrmsgard!]]
- Transformed into a cat while the gang was hunting down [[Bugbear|Bugbears]] in the [[Cragneedle Woods]], ended up leading them to [[Mugwort Snaggletooth|Mugwort]] and [[Wyatt Holloway|Wyatt]]
- Enjoyed [[Mugwort Snaggletooth|Mugwort's]] slop and mead
