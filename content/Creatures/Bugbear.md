*Medium Beast [[Verdant Forest]]*
![[bugbear crew 1.webp]]
###### Overview
- A distant but much more savage relative to the goblin
- What proficiency they have in ferocity, they lack in any semblance of reasoning or cognition
###### Appearances
###### [[Chapter 6 - Meat for Mother]]
- A group was encountered after the gang rescued [[Amara]] from [[Gloomstone Cavern]]
- Showing aggression towards the gang who were already beaten up, they caused [[Liri]] to awaken some form of innate power that she used to scare them away
###### [[Chapter 10.2 - Enter! Wyrmsgard!]]
- A group of three of the red variety stumbled upon and were subsequently killed by the gang in the [[Cragneedle Woods]] while helping [[Murlin]] with his job and finding [[Mugwort Snaggletooth|Mugwort]] and [[Wyatt Holloway|Wyatt]]
- They were drawn to the smell of [[Mugwort Snaggletooth|Mugwort's]] cooking
